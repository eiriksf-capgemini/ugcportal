# Release cost: v0.5.0 vs v0.6.0

What each bead in the two latest releases cost to build and to review, how many review rounds its PR took, what the reviewer found per round, how the planned model tier performed, which of `ugcportal-wzgw`'s five proposals the v0.6.0 numbers support, and what to change for v0.7.0. Written for `ugcportal-7dtn` on 2026-10-07, the day v0.6.0 was cut. The v0.5.0 baseline is `docs/process/release-cost-v0.4.0-vs-v0.5.0.md` (`ugcportal-apsq`) and `docs/process/review-rounds-v0.5.0.md` (`ugcportal-wzgw`).

Every table in the appendix is regenerated by `node scripts/release-cost-report.mjs --releases v0.5.0,v0.6.0` (section 10). Numbers in the prose that the appendix does not print come from the commands quoted in section 10, run over the same `--json` dump; if a figure in the prose disagrees with the appendix, the appendix is right and the prose is stale.

## 1. Read this first: what the figures can and cannot say

- **Totals are lower bounds, but much less so than last time.** `tokens_impl` is recorded on 76 of 85 v0.6.0 beads (32 of 54 in v0.5.0) and `tokens_qa` on 70 (29). The nine without a build figure are beads fixed directly on another bead's PR (`p4jw`, `lasi`, `ufdx`, `bq1k`, `5whs`, `1b88`, `g6n6`, `9v2u`, `f6w3`); their cost sits on the PR's main bead. The totals are therefore close to complete for the first time; the per-bead table still shows those nine as free.
- **`~` marks a figure the bead's own notes call an estimate**: 21 build figures and 1 review figure in v0.6.0 (14 and 2 in v0.5.0). The mark is mechanical, from the words *estimate*, *rough* or *approx* beside a token figure.
- **Per-round review cost is now recorded**, on 68 of the 85 beads, as `tokens_qa_r1`, `tokens_qa_r2`, … This is item 1 of the previous report's "what to record" list, done. The appendix's `qa/round` column still divides `tokens_qa` by the round count, and its "tail" estimate still multiplies that mean, so **where this document gives a per-round figure it uses the recorded `tokens_qa_rN` values (section 3), not the appendix's mean**. The two disagree by a factor of three for later rounds.
- **Two parser faults in the appendix, both visible, both reported in section 8.** `3wcd` (`#121`) shows `r1:121` mediums: the severity parser took the number 121 from the verdict text of a one-round PR that was approved with zero findings. Every medium total that includes it is wrong by 121; section 2 uses a recount that caps a round at 20 and treats that row as zero. And the "Outcome (heuristic)" column reads "human (sensitive path)" on 66 of 77 v0.6.0 PRs because the phrase "no sensitive paths touched" contains "sensitive path". Section 3's merge-path table replaces it with a classification that reads the last round's comment properly.
- **Rounds with no stated severity went from 4 to 22.** The gate keys on the severity the reviewer writes; a round whose verdict names none is shown as `?` and counted as "unstated" below, never inferred. At least 17 of the 22 are **blocking** rounds: verdicts that named findings and stopped the merge without a severity on any of them (`#112` three rounds, `#133` three, `#109` two, and one each on `#107`, `#111`, `#114`, `#115`, `#141`, `#143`, `#158`, `#163`, `#171`); one is an approval and four cannot be told from their text. On every one of those 17 the severity gate did not run, because the value it keys on was never written.
- **Model tier is the planned tier.** v0.6.0 is the first full release built after `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL=sonnet` took effect (2026-09-29) and after `harness-cost-controls` §1 made explicit `model` arguments the rule, so the "sonnet" rows are, for the first time, mostly sonnet runs. The reviewer runs are the exception: section 4 shows Opus's share of subagent spend *rose* between the releases, and the only Opus work routed by policy in v0.6.0 was review.
- **The two windows are not the same length.** v0.5.0 was built 2026-09-29 to 10-05 (seven days, 54 beads). v0.6.0 was built from the evening of 10-05 to midday 10-07 (two days, 85 beads). Per-bead figures are comparable; per-day figures are not, and this document does not use any.
- **Release membership is the CHANGELOG section**, cross-checked against `git log v0.5.0..v0.6.0` (78 commits). Eleven v0.6.0 beads are in the section and not in the git range: all are beads fixed on a sibling's PR or closed without their own commit (the nine above plus `qnq9.2`, the disclosure epic, and `6uxr`, shared with `0sdf` on `#179`). No bead is in the git range and missing from the CHANGELOG this time (two were last time).

1. **Rounds halved and the cap emptied.** The median PR went from four review rounds to two, the mean from 3.69 to 2.11, and the six-round cap was reached once (`nvg0`, `#109`) instead of eleven times. 28 of 70 reviewed PRs were done in one round, 47 in one or two. The sixteen feature PRs have a median of two rounds against 5.5; none reached the cap.
2. **Cost per bead did not move; throughput did.** Recorded cost per bead shipped is 560k against 565k, with 85 beads in two days against 54 in seven. What moved is the split: review fell from 64% to 49% of the recorded total, the median review cost per bead halved (525k to 246k), and the median build cost rose (200k to 296k), partly because 76 beads now record a build figure where 32 did and the newly recorded ones are not the small ones.
3. **Round 1 finds fewer mediums and more PRs pass it clean.** 43% of v0.5.0 round-1 verdicts found a medium; 14% of v0.6.0's did. Zero-finding round-1 merges went from 26% to 33% of reviewed PRs. The stated mediums for the whole release fell from 84 to 30. This is the direction `wzgw` item 2 (pre-review before the PR) predicted; section 6 weighs how much of it the pre-review can claim.
4. **The first round is now the bill.** With `tokens_qa_rN` recorded, the split the previous report had to estimate can be measured: round 1 costs a median 185k (67k to 980k) and a later round a median 60k, 0.29 of its own PR's round 1, under half of it on 74% of later rounds. Summed over the 68 beads with per-round figures, round 1 is 15.5M of 22.9M review tokens (68%) and all later rounds together 7.4M (32%). The previous report's crude mean put the tail at 38%; the recorded figures say the tail is cheaper than that and the opening round dearer.
5. **Severity discipline slipped.** 22 of 148 rounds state no severity, against 4 of 129, and at least 17 of the 22 are blocking rounds — findings named, merge stopped, no severity written (`#112` and `#133` three rounds each, `#109` two, nine more PRs one each). On those the severity gate did not run, because the value it keys on was never there; `#133` then merged "under the round-4 rule with one low filed" on an `approx` chain, which has no round-4 rule. `z1nh`'s close reason records the other half of the same slip: an orchestrator framed round 4 as lenient on an `approx` chain.
6. **Humans still merged 27 of 77 PRs, for a different reason than before.** In v0.5.0, 18 of the 24 human merges were cap escalations or blocked last rounds. In v0.6.0, 17 of 20 were sensitive-path PRs (`.claude/skills`, `.github/workflows`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docker-compose.yml`), three were cap or blocked, and seven process PRs (`#104`, `#105`, `#106`, `#113`, `#127`, `#136`, `#137`) merged with no review round at all. The identity change (`wzgw` item 1, still open as `ugcportal-62b6`) would have saved three rounds in this release — the lows-only rounds at round 4 and 5 — against 20 in the previous one.
7. **Features cost half.** Sixteen feature beads recorded 7.65M build and 4.13M review, 737k per feature, against 1.51M per feature in v0.5.0 (15.1M over ten). The design epic's four feature children (`qqnt.1`, `qqnt.3`, `qqnt.4`, `qqnt.5`) ran 1 to 2 rounds each, 3.07M combined, and its refactor child `qqnt.2` four rounds and 1.39M; the disclosure epic's four (`qnq9.1`, `qnq9.2.1`, `qnq9.2.2`, `e0jv`) 1 to 3 rounds and 3.15M.
8. **Opus's share of spend rose, because review runs on it.** The harness measurement (`usage_indicators.py`, list price) puts Opus at 38% of subagent spend for 10-06 to 10-07 ($730 of $1,910) against ~25% in the v0.5.0 window. Nine of 85 beads were planned on opus; the previous report's routing table (its section 6.1) put round 1 and the cap round of *review* on opus/xhigh, and that, not the implementers, is where the share came from. Finding 4 says the first round is 68% of review cost; this says that round is mostly Opus.

## 3. Where the review tokens go

### 3.1 Round economics, by round number

Each unique PR once (`#155` is shared by `cr2h`, `p4jw`, `lasi`; `#158` by `8hsf`, `ufdx`, `1b88`, `g6n6`; `#179` by `0sdf`, `6uxr`). A round is "unstated" when its verdict names no severity.

| Round | Rounds run | Found a medium | Lows only | Nothing found | Severity unstated |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 70 | 10 | 19 | 26 | 15 |
| 2 | 42 | 8 | 11 | 20 | 3 |
| 3 | 23 | 4 | 9 | 8 | 2 |
| 4 | 10 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 2 |
| 5 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 6 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **All** | **148** | **25** | **42** | **59** | **22** |

The v0.5.0 table for comparison (from the same run; the previous report's figures, same method): 129 rounds, 50 with a medium, 58 lows only, 17 nothing, 4 unstated; rounds 5-6 ran 30 times and found a medium 4 times.

Three things follow.

- **The lows-only tail is gone; a verification tail replaced it.** Rounds at or after 4 ran 13 times (50 in v0.5.0): 3 found a medium, 3 lows only, 5 nothing, 2 unstated. But 59 of 148 rounds found nothing at all, against 17 of 129 — 40% of rounds against 13%. Those are the round after a one-line fix: `#173` round 2 verifying a reworded docstring ("exactly one file, one hunk, comment-only", the verdict says); `#176` round 3 after two sentences written during round 2's fix; 20 of the 42 second rounds found nothing. The round is correct — the strict band blocks on any finding and the next round must confirm the fix — and it is now the commonest round shape.
- **A later round costs a third of the first.** Recorded `tokens_qa_rN`: later rounds median 60k against round-1 median 185k, ratio 0.29 per PR, 74% of later rounds under half their round 1. The whole later-round bill is 7.4M (32% of review). So the "fresh agent re-verifies the whole PR each round" cost structure the previous report described (its cause 3) is **no longer what the figures show**: a delta round is already cheap. What is not cheap is round 1 — 15.5M, 68% — and the nine PRs whose round 1 cost over 300k (`#129` 980k, `#133` 871k, `#139` 610k, `#128` 609k, `#146` 516k, `#117` 512k, `#119` 471k, `#115` 413k, `#132` 381k) are where the review budget went. These were the reviewer running the full suite, the guard suites and the real flow on a fresh worktree against a large diff; that is what round 1 is for, and it is the right place to look for cost, not the tail.
- **Blocking on prose still costs a round, at a third of the price.** `#176` round 2: "both new findings are sentences written *during this fix round* — the `review-standards` §5 pattern — and both are one-line edits". `#173` round 1: one confirmed low, a stale empty-state docstring. Each cost an implementer pass and a verification round. `review-standards` §5 (comment discipline, `wzgw` item 4) is the rule they broke, and the pre-review's claims audit the tool that should have caught them before the push; on these two PRs it did not, because the sentences were written *after* the pre-review ran, in the fix round itself.

### 3.2 Implementation passes

`tokens_impl_passes` is set on 61 of the 85 beads: 16 beads took one pass, 21 two, 15 three, 5 four, 3 five (`ff2a`, `qnq9.3`, `qqnt.4`) and 1 seven (`nvg0`). The previous report's budget rule B2 ("three implementation passes without a clean round: stop") would have fired on nine beads. Two of `qqnt.4`'s five passes were merge-conflict resolutions against `#175` and `#177`, not fixes — the cost `wzgw` item 5 moves from a red `main` to the implementer.

## 5. The five most expensive beads of v0.6.0, and one reduction each

Ranked by recorded total. "Round 1" and "later rounds" are the recorded `tokens_qa_rN` values, not the appendix mean.

| # | Bead (PR) | Total | Build (passes) | Review (rounds) | Round 1 | Later rounds | Mediums by round |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | `61pv` reduced-motion scan: residual shapes and the e2e directory (`#129`) | ~1.69M | ~587k (2) | 1,099k (2) | 980k | 119k | r1:1 r2:2 |
| 2 | `np1i` claims-audit false all-clean on uncommitted files (`#133`) | 1.62M | 423k (3) | 1,200k (4) | 871k | 67k, 152k, 110k | r1:? r2:3 r3:? r4:? |
| 3 | `nvg0` PR close deletes the merged branch and prunes the worktree (`#109`, `#113`) | 1.53M | 793k (7) | 742k (6) | — | — | r3:1 r4:3 r5:1 r6:1 |
| 4 | `ff2a` Try again button drops keyboard focus (`#112`) | 1.40M | 594k (5) | 805k (4) | 276k | 190k, 188k, 151k | r1:? r2:? r3:? |
| 5 | `qqnt.2` one button system on the public pages (`#122`) | ~1.39M | ~886k (4) | 507k (4) | — | — | r1:2 r2:1 |

1. **`61pv`: the most expensive single review round of the release, 980k, on a P4 test bead.** A reduced-motion CSS scan whose round 1 found one medium and round 2 found two more. The round-1 figure is five times the release median; it bought a reviewer that compiled Tailwind to check what `@source not` excludes, which is the "drive the real flow" step. Reduction: that compile is a one-off fact, and the bead's own `check-reduced-motion-css-exclusions.mjs` now asserts it — the pre-review (step 2, guard suites) should have run it on the branch so round 1 reads a green script instead of rebuilding the argument. A P4 bead with a 587k build and a 1.1M review is also the shape budget B4 (1.5× the shape's budget) exists to stop; nothing stopped it.
2. **`np1i`: four rounds, three of them with no stated severity, and the 871k first round.** A fix to `claims-audit` itself; round 2 stated three mediums, rounds 1, 3 and 4 stated none, and the PR merged under "the round-4 rule with one low filed" (`aigs`) — on an `approx` chain, which has no round-4 rule. Reduction: the severity line on every finding (section 9, item 1). Had rounds 3 and 4 stated "low", the gate reads the same way and the record is honest; had they stated "medium", the merge was wrong. Nobody can tell from the PR which it was.
3. **`nvg0`: the only cap PR, seven implementation passes, twenty findings over six rounds, every medium "a real way to lose a commit or a branch".** Its close reason is the best argument in the release for the strict band on this kind of change, and the best argument for B2: after pass three the remaining rounds were redesign in review (unpushed-commit guard, prune handling, a live re-check before each `branch -D`). Reduction: the bead was under-specified for a destructive script — "delete the merged branch" became a classifier with four guards. Write the invariant first (`z1nh`'s lesson, section 7): which refs may never be deleted, then the script.
4. **`ff2a`: four rounds and five passes with no severity stated on any blocking round.** The close reason says "the one regression along the way came from fixing a plausible low in flight". Reduction: same as 2, plus the rule the close reason implies — on an `approx` chain a plausible low still blocks, so fix it, but do not touch anything else in the same pass.
5. **`qqnt.2`: 886k of build over four passes for a design refactor whose rounds found three mediums (two at round 1, one at round 2) and 14 low labels.** Merged "after Eirik chose to fix the forced-colors focus at the two call sites", a product decision taken at round 4 that could have been taken at bead-authoring time. Reduction: a design bead that touches shared components should name its out-of-scope callers in the bead, as this one eventually did ("out-of-scope callers byte-identical").

For contrast, v0.5.0's top five were 2.07M to 2.51M each, all five or six rounds, two to four of them lows-only. v0.6.0's top five are 1.39M to 1.69M, two to six rounds, and the cost is in the opening round and the implementation passes, not the tail.

## 7. Process incidents in the release, and what each cost

Each of these is in a bead's notes or close reason; none is in the token figures as its own line.

- **Finder forks writing to live PRs and generating load (`ugcportal-cr2h`, `#155`, two rounds, 703k).** `code-review`'s finder forks posted inline comments and a "write access test" comment before any verdict, reported to the wrong agent, ran redundant builds in a shared worktree, and one ran `pkill -9 -f "next build"` machine-wide. Fixed by taking the fork out: `pr-review-merge` now reviews inline in one agent, posts nothing before its verdict, and runs a pre-stamp integrity check for writes it did not make. Subsumed `p4jw` and `lasi`. The bd memory `never-start-load-generators-in-agent-briefs` is the same family.
- **Substring `pkill` killing a sibling's dev server (`qqnt.4`, pass 2).** `pkill -f "node_modules/.bin/next dev"` from one worktree killed another worktree's server (finished, so no work lost). The rule "kill only your own processes, by PID" was already in the brief. The lesson is that a prose rule in a brief does not stop a substring kill; the reviewer skill now says the same thing in its step 4 rules, and the implementer brief should too.
- **Two orchestrator briefs that omitted the bead's own criteria (`qqnt.4`, `0sdf`).** `qqnt.4`'s first brief told the implementer to keep the decorative circles the bead's K1 replaces; `0sdf`'s brief named three lows and left out its K2 (one test per `isPlainLeftClick` clause). Both implementers did what the brief said and declared the rest out of scope; both were sent back before review (one extra pass each, `0sdf`'s 111k). Rule from both notes: *briefs quote the bead's acceptance criteria verbatim, never a paraphrased subset*.
- **Shape instead of invariant (`z1nh`, `#182`, 4 rounds, 4 passes, 807k, a P4).** A guard specified by the shape of one bug (a `text-petrol-900` that compiles to nothing) grew round by round into the invariant it should have started as (every literal colour-utility token compiles, 217 today). The close reason also records the orchestrator "wrongly framed round 4 as lenient; an approx chain stays in the strict band at every round".
- **Blocking on sentences written during the fix (`#173` r1, `#176` r2, `#183` r1 unstated, `#180` two approvals).** Section 3.1. Each cost one implementer pass and one ~60k verification round.
- **Stale notifications from killed polling loops.** Named in the bead's description from the orchestrator's own experience; there is no figure for it and this document does not invent one. The mechanism is in the harness (a background `--watch` whose process was killed keeps delivering completion events), and the only cost is orchestrator attention.
- **The `approx`-chain strict band (`ugcportal-62b6`, open).** Still in force, still correct given a single identity. Section 6, item 1: its price fell to three rounds in this release.

## 8. Data gaps found while producing this

## 9. Process changes for v0.7.0 (K3)

Each tied to a measured cause above; in the order the numbers rank them.

1. **A severity on every finding, and a report that refuses to count without one.** Cause: 22 unstated rounds (15%) against 4 (3%); at least 17 of them blocking rounds whose gate could not run, across 14 PRs; one merge "under the round-4 rule" on a chain that has none. Change: `pr-review-merge`'s blocking template gains a per-finding `severity:` field that is not optional, and `release-cost-report.mjs` prints unstated blocking rounds as a failure in its data-gaps section rather than a footnote.
2. **Spend the first round where it earns, not on a fresh full pass for every diff size.** Cause: round 1 is 68% of review (15.5M); eleven first rounds over 300k; `61pv`'s 980k round 1 on a P4. Change: the first-round depth follows the bead's shape budget from the previous report's section 6.2 (a docs/test/mechanical bead gets a 150k review budget, which `61pv`, `np1i`, `ymp4`, `9faa` and `96eb` all exceeded in round 1), and the orchestrator reports the breach instead of paying it. Resuming the reviewer (item 3) stays shelved: later rounds are already at 0.29.
3. **Briefs quote the bead's criteria verbatim.** Cause: two briefs that paraphrased a subset (`qqnt.4`, `0sdf`), two extra passes. Change: the brief template carries the bead's `K1..Kn` text as a block, copied, not summarised.
4. **B2 is enforced: three implementation passes without a clean round, stop and re-plan.** Cause: nine beads over three passes; `nvg0` seven, `ff2a`, `qnq9.3`, `qqnt.4` five; two of the three cap-or-blocked human merges (`#112` five passes, `#115` four) were over three. Change: the orchestrator checks `tokens_impl_passes` before spawning a fourth pass.
5. **Fix the report's two parsers before the next cut.** Cause: section 8, items 1 and 2; the appendix tables of this very document carry the wrong numbers in two columns.
6. **Decide, in writing, whether process-doc PRs are reviewed.** Cause: seven PRs merged with no round. Either `pr-review-merge` runs on them (and the human still merges, since they are sensitive paths) or `CLAUDE.md` says they are exempt and why.
7. **Keep the identity change (`62b6`) but as a latency fix, not a cost fix.** Cause: three rounds and three human merges it would have saved; its value in v0.5.0 was 20 rounds. It still removes the overnight wait on a cap escalation, and that is the argument for it now.
8. **Route delta rounds to sonnet.** Cause: Opus 38% of subagent spend with nine opus beads in 85; the routing table already says delta rounds may be sonnet. The orchestrator names `model` on the reviewer spawn for rounds 2+ and the harness measurement for v0.7.0 should show the Opus share back near 25%.

The round table, merge-path classification, feature medians and recorded per-round costs in sections 2, 3 and 6 come from this script over the same two files (`rows[release]` are the beads, `prs[number].rounds[]` the per-round verdicts with `medium`, `low` and `stated`):

```bash
python3 - <<'EOF'
import json, statistics as st
from collections import Counter, defaultdict
d = json.load(open('/tmp/release-cost-v0.6.0.json')); rows, prs = d['rows'], d['prs']
beads = json.load(open('/tmp/beads.json'))
for rel in ('v0.5.0', 'v0.6.0'):
    prset = {n for r in rows[rel] for n in (r.get('prs') or [])}
    counted = {n: prs[str(n)]['rounds'] for n in prset if str(n) in prs and prs[str(n)]['rounds']}
    vals = sorted(len(v) for v in counted.values())
    print(rel, 'PRs', len(prset), 'with rounds', len(counted), 'median', st.median(vals), 'mean', round(st.mean(vals), 2), 'cap', sum(v >= 6 for v in vals))
    tab = defaultdict(Counter)
    for rounds in counted.values():
        for rd in rounds:
            k = 'unstated' if not rd['stated'] else 'medium' if rd['medium'] else 'lows' if rd['low'] else 'nothing'
            tab[rd['n']][k] += 1; tab[rd['n']]['run'] += 1
    for n in sorted(tab): print('  round', n, dict(tab[n]))
    cls = Counter()
    for n in sorted(prset):
        p = prs.get(str(n)); b = (p.get('lastRoundBody') or '').lower() if p else ''
        if not p or not p['rounds']: cls['no review round'] += 1
        elif 'sensitive path' in b and 'no sensitive path' not in b: cls['human: sensitive path'] += 1
        elif any(k in b for k in ('auto-approved', 'merging', 'approved', 'no blocking findings')) and 'not merging' not in b: cls['merged by the skill'] += 1
        else: cls['human: cap or blocked'] += 1
    print('  merge path', dict(cls))
    feat = {n for r in rows[rel] if r['ccType'] == 'feat' for n in (r.get('prs') or [])}
    print('  feature PR rounds', sorted(len(counted[n]) for n in feat if n in counted))
    print('  mediums (capped 20/round)', sum(min(rd['medium'], 20) for v in counted.values() for rd in v), 'low labels', sum(rd['low'] for v in counted.values() for rd in v))
ids = {r['id'] for r in rows['v0.6.0']}
r1, later, ratio = [], [], []
for b in beads:
    if b['id'] not in ids: continue
    m = {k: int(v) for k, v in (b.get('metadata') or {}).items() if k.startswith('tokens_qa_r') and k[11:].isdigit() and str(v).isdigit()}
    if 'tokens_qa_r1' in m:
        r1.append(m['tokens_qa_r1'])
        for k, v in m.items():
            if k != 'tokens_qa_r1': later.append(v); ratio.append(v / m['tokens_qa_r1'])
print('round 1: n', len(r1), 'median', int(st.median(r1)), 'sum', sum(r1), '| later: n', len(later), 'median', int(st.median(later)), 'sum', sum(later), '| ratio median', round(st.median(ratio), 2), 'under 0.5', round(sum(x < 0.5 for x in ratio) / len(ratio), 2))
EOF
```
